Batman, alias Bruce Wayne, is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by artist Bob Kane and writer Bill Finger, he first debuted in Detective Comics #27 in 1939. Driven by the tragic childhood murder of his parents, Thomas and Martha Wayne, Bruce dedicates his life to waging a relentless war on crime. Unlike most superheroes, Batman possesses no superhuman superpowers. Instead, he relies on his genius intellect, master-level martial arts skills, peak physical conditioning, and vast wealth to fund an arsenal of high-tech weaponry and vehicles, most notably the Batmobile. Operating from the shadows of Gotham City, Batman guards his secret identity while maintaining a dual life as a billionaire playboy and philanthropist. He operates out of the Batcave beneath Wayne Manor and is supported by key allies, including his loyal butler Alfred Pennyworth, Police Commissioner James Gordon, and his sidekick Robin. Batman fights an infamous rogues' gallery of villains, led by his archenemy, the Joker. Known as the Dark Knight and the World’s Greatest Detective, Batman stands as a global cultural icon of justice, resilience, and human determination.

Superman, alter ego Clark Kent, is a legendary superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Jerry Siegel and artist Joe Shuster, he made his monumental debut in Action Comics #1 in 1938. Born Kal-El on the doomed planet Krypton, he was sent to Earth by his biological parents just before his home world exploded. His rocket crash-landed in Smallville, Kansas, where he was adopted and raised with strong moral values by farmers Jonathan and Martha Kent. Under the radiation of Earth’s yellow sun, Superman possesses god-like superpowers, including immense super strength, invulnerability, flight, super speed, heat vision, and x-ray vision. To protect his loved ones, he lives a dual life in Metropolis as a mild-mannered journalist for the Daily Planet. He is supported by his main love interest and fellow reporter Lois Lane, and photographer Jimmy Olsen, while operating out of his secret sanctuary, the Fortress of Solitude. Superman protects humanity from formidable threats, most notably his archenemy, the power-hungry billionaire Lex Luthor, while remaining vulnerable to magic and the radioactive remnants of his home planet, Kryptonite. Widely recognized as the Man of Steel, Superman serves as the ultimate global symbol of hope, truth, and justice.
